Important Clinical Disclaimer

This web application is an educational screening and interpretation tool designed to help community participants understand numbers printed by an automated autorefractometer. It is NOT an official diagnosis, medical opinion, or spectacle prescription. Eye health involves corneal curvature, intraocular pressure, retinal wellness, and subjective visual comfort. Never purchase eyeglasses solely based on automated screening numbers without an examination by a certified optometrist or ophthalmologist.

Quick 1-Minute Google Sheets Setup:

  1. Open a new blank sheet on Google Sheets.
  2. Click Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete the default code, paste the contents of google-sheets-script.js from this folder, and click Save.
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment, select Web app, set Who has access to Anyone, and click Deploy.
  5. Copy the resulting Web App URL and paste it into the box above!
